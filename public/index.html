🇮🇳 हिंदी अर्थ
जो व्यक्ति खाने-पीने, सोने-जागने, काम करने में सही मात्रा का पालन करता है, उसका योग सभी दुखों का नाश करने वाला बन जाता है।
🌍 English Translation
"He who is regulated in his habits of eating, sleeping, recreation and work can mitigate all material pains by practicing the yoga system."
✨ Reflective Insight
This verse serves as an ancient guide to mindful moderation. True wellness is not about extreme asceticism or endless indulgence, but finding the sacred equilibrium. Today, focus on keeping your workload balanced and your sleep habits steady.
🌱 Practice for Today
Before eating a meal today, stop for 5 seconds to express gratitude and eat without screen distractions. Observe the natural balance.

Common Questions

Everything you need to know about the daily wisdom subscription

Which texts are covered in the daily emails? ▼
We rotate daily shlokas across the Bhagavad Gita, Valmiki Ramayan, and principal Upanishads (like Katha, Mundaka, Isha). Every select verse is chosen for its profound, universal, and practical wisdom applicable to modern daily struggles.
Can I change my preferred delivery time later? ▼
Absolutely! Once you subscribe, you receive a personal subscriber link in every email. From your dashboard, you can change your delivery time slot (Sunrise, Brahma Muhurta, Evening, etc.) instantly with one click.
How does the AI reflection work? ▼
We use advanced AI to analyze the traditional Sanskrit commentary and map its core teachings to practical modern contexts. Instead of just reading ancient text, the AI provides actionable insights regarding focus, work-life balance, stress management, relationships, and daily productivity.
Is it easy to cancel or unsubscribe? ▼
Yes, we believe in complete transparency and peace of mind. Every email contains a link to manage your settings. From your profile page, you can easily update your preferences or confirm deactivation at any time.
What payment methods are supported? ▼
We accept all major payment methods processed securely through Razorpay. This includes UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm), Credit and Debit Cards (Visa, Mastercard, RuPay), Netbanking, and popular wallets.
